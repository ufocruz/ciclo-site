# CicloBem help

Quick answers to the most common questions. Didn't find yours? Write to [cruzufo@gmail.com](mailto:cruzufo@gmail.com).

## How do I log my period?

On the **Today** screen, tap the big button when your period starts and again when it ends. If it started on
another day, use **"Started on another day"**, right below the button. Past dates can be added, fixed or deleted in
**History** or by tapping the days in the **Calendar**.

## How do I add the widget to the home screen?

- **Android:** touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose **Widgets** and look for CicloBem.
- **iPhone (iOS 17 or later):** touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap **Edit** or **+**, and look
  for CicloBem.

The widget button logs the start and the end without opening the app. On the day you log it, it shows **Undo**: an
extra tap by mistake undoes the entry instead of logging another one.

## How do I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language (Portuguese, English or Spanish; for any other language, English). To choose
another one, go to **Settings > Language**.

## The prediction is wrong. What do I do?

Predictions are estimates based on your history. They get better after a few logged cycles. Check in **History**
that the dates are right: a forgotten start, or one logged on the wrong day, changes the prediction.

This app is not a contraceptive method and does not replace medical advice.

## Reminders don't arrive

- Check that CicloBem's notifications are allowed in your phone settings.
- Check in **Settings > Reminders** that the reminder type is turned on.
- On Android, battery saving can delay notifications. If that happens, exclude CicloBem from battery optimization.

## I changed phones. How do I move my history over?

- **System backup:** if iCloud backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is on, your history may come back by itself
  when you install the app on the new phone, with the same account.
- **Export and import:** on the old phone, use **Settings > Export backup** and keep the file. On the new one, use
  **Settings > Import backup**. Importing replaces what is in the app.

We don't keep a copy of your history, so we can't recover it for you.

## I bought "Remove ads" and they still show up

Open **Settings > Ads** and tap **Restore purchase**, while connected to the internet and signed in with the
same store account you used for the purchase. If the payment is still pending, ads go away when the store confirms it.

Refund requests are made directly to the store (App Store or Google Play), which processes the payment.

## How do I delete my data?

In **Settings > Delete all data**. Uninstalling the app also deletes the data on the phone. Details in the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).
