# Ciclo privacy policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Ciclo is a period tracking app that works **with no account, no login and no server**. This policy explains, in
plain words, what happens to your data.

## Summary

- Your history and your settings stay **only on your phone**.
- We, the people who make the app, **have no access** to any of your cycle data.
- The app shows **non-personalized** ads. The ad service never receives anything about your cycle.
- If you email us, we use your address only to reply.
- You can delete everything at any time.

## What the app stores, why and where

The app stores on the phone itself:

- the start and end dates of your periods and the cycles you confirmed, to show the calendar, predictions, analysis
  and reminders;
- your settings: reminder time and types, discreet mode, language, initial cycle length, whether ads were removed and
  whether the app has already asked for notification permission;
- month and year of birth, only if you provide them. They are used so the analysis applies the cycle range expected
  for people under 18.

This data stays in a private area of the app on your phone until you delete it. None of it is sent to us or to anyone
else. Since the app opens without a password, anyone who unlocks your phone can open it: use a screen lock.

**System backup:** if iCloud backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is turned on, the system itself
may copy the app's files to **your** account, as it does with other apps. This lets you recover your history when
you change phones. We have no access to that backup. You can turn it off in your phone settings.

**Export backup:** if you use "Export backup" in Settings, the app creates a file with your history and opens the
phone's share screen. You choose where it goes. The file is not password-protected; keep it somewhere safe.

## Reminders and widget

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the phone itself. The widget reads the same files as the app.
Neither of them uses the internet.

## Ads

The free version shows **Google AdMob** banners. For that, Google's SDK receives technical data from the device, such
as model, operating system, language, IP address (which shows your approximate location), technical identifiers other
than the advertising ID, crash and performance data, and ad usage information. The ad request also says which app the
ad will appear in. This data is used to deliver ads, measure impressions and prevent fraud.

What the app does to limit this:

- it requests **only non-personalized ads**, for everyone;
- it **does not use the advertising identifier**: on iPhone the app does not ask for tracking permission; on
  Android the advertising ID permission is removed;
- it limits ads to Google's PG content rating, suitable for teenagers;
- it **never** passes to Google any data about your cycle, your settings or the screens you use.

Google handles this data as a controller, under its own rules. We do not receive this data: the AdMob dashboard only
shows us total impressions and revenue. Google may store it on servers outside Brazil, including in the United
States, with the safeguards it describes, and for the period set in its policy.

**In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, the app tags every ad request as coming from
someone under the age of consent. The app is for people aged 13 and over, and in several of these countries you can
only consent without your parents from age 16. So the app does not ask for ad consent there, and Google shows ads
without personalization and without calling other ad or measurement companies.

Learn more:

- how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- how long Google keeps data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

## "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase is made and processed by the App Store or Google Play, with your store account. We do not receive your
payment details. The app only asks the store whether the purchase exists and keeps that information on the phone.

## If you email us

If you send us an email, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we delete it
once the matter is closed, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation. Do not send your cycle data or your
backup file: we do not need them to help.

## Legal bases

- **Cycle data and settings:** they never reach us; they stay on your phone, under your control.
- **Ads:** in Brazil and most countries, our legitimate interest in keeping the app free (LGPD, art. 7, IX), with
  non-personalized ads only, to reduce the impact. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and
  Switzerland, legitimate interest (GDPR, art. 6(1)(f)), with the restricted processing described under "Ads".
- **Purchase:** performance of the contract (LGPD, art. 7, V).
- **Email:** our legitimate interest in replying to you (art. 7, IX) and, when it is a request about your rights,
  compliance with a legal obligation (art. 7, II).

## Children and teenagers

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. It has no sign-up, no social features, no sharing between users and
does not collect cycle data. No one gets personalized ads or is profiled for advertising, in line with the LGPD
(art. 14) and Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law 15,211/2025). If we learn that we received by
email data from a child under 13 without the consent of their parent or guardian, we delete it.

## How to delete your data

- In **Settings > Delete all data**, the app deletes the history, the settings (except the language and the "Remove
  ads" purchase), the internal safety copies, the scheduled reminders and the widget data.
- Uninstalling the app also deletes the data on the phone.
- Backups you exported, or that the system made to your account, stay with you; delete them there if you want.

## Your rights

The app is made in Brazil, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018). Under the LGPD
(art. 18), you have the right to confirm whether we process your data, access it, correct it, ask for it to be
anonymized, blocked or deleted, take it to another service, know who it is shared with, withdraw consent and object
to processing.

Since your cycle data stays only on your phone, you are the one in control: you can see, correct, export and delete
everything in the app itself. For email data, or for questions about this policy or the data handled by the ad SDK,
contact us using the details below; we reply within 15 days. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection
Authority (ANPD): https://www.gov.br/anpd

**In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, you have the rights of access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you can complain to the data protection authority in your
country.
