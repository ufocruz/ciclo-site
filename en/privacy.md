# Ciclo privacy policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Ciclo is a period tracking app that works **with no account, no login and no server**. This policy explains, in
plain words, what happens to your data.

## Summary

- Your history and your settings stay **only on your phone**.
- We, the people who make the app, **have no access** to any of your cycle data.
- The app shows **non-personalized** ads. The ad service never receives anything about your cycle.
- You can delete everything at any time.

## What the app stores and where

The app stores on the phone itself:

- the start and end dates of your periods and the cycles you confirmed;
- your settings: reminder time and types, discreet mode, language, initial cycle length, month and year of birth (if
  you provide them), whether ads were removed and whether the app has already asked for notification permission.

This data stays in a private area of the app on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

**System backup:** if iCloud backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is turned on, the system itself
may copy the app's files to **your** account, as it does with other apps. This lets you recover your history when
you change phones. We have no access to that backup. You can turn it off in your phone settings.

**Export backup:** if you use "Export backup" in Settings, the app creates a file with your history and opens the
phone's share screen. You choose where it goes. The file is not password-protected; keep it somewhere safe.

## Reminders and widget

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the phone itself. The widget reads the same files as the app.
Neither of them uses the internet.

## Ads

The free version shows **Google AdMob** banners. For that, Google's SDK receives technical data from the device, such
as model, operating system, language, IP address and ad usage information, to deliver ads, measure impressions and
prevent fraud.

What the app does to limit this:

- it requests **only non-personalized ads**, for everyone;
- it **does not use the advertising identifier**: on iPhone the app does not ask for tracking permission; on
  Android the advertising ID permission is removed;
- it limits ads to content suitable for teenagers;
- it **never** passes to Google any data about your cycle, your settings or the screens you use.

In countries where the law requires consent (such as the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the
app shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy.

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase is made and processed by the App Store or Google Play, with your store account. We do not receive your
payment details. The app only asks the store whether the purchase exists and keeps that information on the phone.

## Children and teenagers

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. It has no social features, no sharing between users and does not
collect cycle data. Ads are non-personalized for everyone.

## How to delete your data

- In **Settings > Delete all data**, the app deletes the history, the settings (except the language and the "Remove
  ads" purchase), the internal safety copies, the scheduled reminders and the widget data.
- Uninstalling the app also deletes the data on the phone.
- Backups you exported, or that the system made to your account, stay with you; delete them there if you want.

## Your rights

The app is made in Brazil, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Since your cycle data stays only
on your phone and we have no access to it, you are the one in control: you can see, correct, export and delete
everything in the app itself. For questions about this policy or about the data handled by the ad SDK, contact us
using the details below.
